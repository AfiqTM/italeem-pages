Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy covers the iTa’leem app for Android and iOS (“the app”). The app is an unofficial, personal project and is not affiliated with any university.

What the app does with your data

The app runs on your phone. The developer does not operate a server for it and does not receive, collect, sell or share your personal data.

  • iTa’leem account: you sign in on the iTa’leem website itself. The app keeps the access token the site gives it in secure storage on your device so you stay signed in.
  • Course data: announcements, assignments, grades, attendance and similar content are downloaded from the iTa’leem site to your device for offline use. They stay on your device.

Google user data

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app asks for access to Google Drive (the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive scope). It uses that access only to:

  • list, search, download and open files you select in your Drive;
  • read and write one file, iTaleem-sync.json, in the root of your Drive, which stores your to-do list, saved marks, pins and saved-file shortcuts so the app and the website userscript stay in sync.

The Google access token is kept on your device. Your Drive files and the sync file go directly between your device and Google. They are never sent to the developer or any third party.

The app’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not allow humans to read it.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Sign out of Drive inside the app (Others › My Drive › account menu) to delete the stored Google token from your device.
  • Revoke the app’s access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete iTaleem-sync.json from your Google Drive to remove the synced data.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Children

The app is intended for students and is not directed at children under 13.
