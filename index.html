iTa’leem

A personal mobile app for the iTa’leem learning site, with optional Google Drive sync.

iTa’leem is an independent, unofficial app made by one student developer. It gives you a faster, app-style view of your courses, assignments, alerts and attendance from the iTa’leem website, and works offline with the data it has already downloaded.

Google Drive

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app can:

  • let you browse and open your own Drive files inside the app;
  • keep your to-do list, saved marks and pinned files in sync between the app and the iTa’leem userscript, using one small file called iTaleem-sync.json in your Drive.

Signing in with Google is optional. Everything else in the app works without it.
